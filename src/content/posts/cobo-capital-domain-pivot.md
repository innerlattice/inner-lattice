> **Thesis**: The organization operates by managing and transforming five distinct forms of Capital. This view reveals the ecosystem of value exchange.

---

## 1. Financial Capital
*The liquid energy of the system.*

### 1.1 External (In/Out)
*   **Ingestor**: **7.1.3 Treasury** (accessing Capital Markets, Lines of Credit) & **4.3.1 Sales** (Revenue Capture).
*   **Extruder**: **7.1.2 Accounting** (Tax payments, Dividends).

### 1.2 Internal (Flow/Store)
*   **Distributor**: **7.1.1 FP&A** (Budget Allocation, Capital Efficiency Modeling).
*   **Storage**: Bank Accounts, General Ledger.

### 1.3 Capital Transforms
*   *Cash -> Physical*: **6.2.1 Procurement** (Purchasing assets).
*   *Cash -> Human*: **11.1.1 Acquisition** (Paying salaries/bonuses).
*   *Cash -> Growth*: **4.2.2 Demand Gen** (Ad Spend -> Leads).

### 1.4 Meta-Layer
*   **Decisions & History**: **1.1.3 Resource Allocation**, Historical Audit Logs.
*   **Situational Awareness**: **1.1.1 Market Analysis**, Cash position monitoring.
*   **Procedures**: Expense Policy, Delegated Authority Matrix.
*   **Security**: Fraud Detection, Financial Controls (SOX).
*   **Philosophy**: Risk Tolerance, Investment Thesis.

---

## 2. Code Capital (Intellectual & Product)
*The crystallized logic and value of the organization.*

### 2.1 External (In/Out)
*   **External**: **10.1.1 Tech Partnerships** (OSS Contributions, 3rd Party APIs).
*   **Extruder**: **3.2.1 Release Engineering** (Binaries, SDKs), **10.1.3 DevRel** (Documentation).

### 2.2 Internal (Flow/Store)
*   **Producer**: **3.4.1 Feature Build**, **3.1.1 System Design**.
*   **Storage**: **3.2.2 Code Repositories** (Version Control), Container Registries.

### 2.3 Capital Transforms
*   *Spec -> Code*: **15. Decoder** (PRD -> Implementation).
*   *Code -> User Value*: **19. Encoder** (UI/UX Rendering).
*   *Code -> Reliability*: **3.2.3 SRE** (Code -> Uptime).

### 2.4 Meta-Layer
*   **Decisions & History**: Architecture RFCs, Git Commit History.
*   **Situational Awareness**: **3.1.2 Tech Health** (Observability, Debt monitors).
*   **Procedures**: CI/CD Pipelines, Style Guides, PR Review Policy.
*   **Security**: **9.1.1 Product Security** (AppSec, SAST/DAST).
*   **Philosophy**: Engineering Principles (e.g., "Move Fast," "Don't Repeat Yourself").

---

## 3. Physical & Hardware Capital
*The material substrate.*

### 3.1 External (In/Out)
*   **External**: Logistics Providers, Cloud Providers (AWS/GCP as rented hardware).
*   **Extruder**: **11.1.1 Offboarding** (Asset retrieval).

### 3.2 Internal (Flow/Store)
*   **Distributor**: **6.2.2 Environment Logistics** (Workspace & Asset distribution).
*   **Storage**: Warehouses, Inventory Closets, Server Racks.

### 3.3 Capital Transforms
*   *Hardware -> Compute*: Data Center Operations (Electricity + Silicon -> Processing Power).

### 3.4 Meta-Layer
*   **Decisions & History**: Procurement Strategy, Asset Depreciation Schedules.
*   **Situational Awareness**: Asset Inventory Database.
*   **Procedures**: Equipment Request Process, Physical Access Policy.
*   **Security**: **9.1.2 Infrastructure Security**, Badge Access.
*   **Philosophy**: Sustainability Goals, Remote-First vs Office-Centric.

---

## 4. Human & Agent Capital
*The active intelligence (Biomass and Synthetic Intelligence).*

### 4.1 External (In/Out)
*   **Ingestor**: **11.1.1 Talent Acquisition** (Candidates), **10.1.2 Agency Partners**.
*   **External**: **11.1.1 Offboarding** (Alumni).

### 4.2 Internal (Flow/Store)
*   **Storage**: Employee Directory, Agent Registry level.
*   **Distributor**: **6.1.2 Org Design** (Team formation).

### 4.3 Capital Transforms
*   *Potential -> Capability*: **11.1.2 Learning & Development** (Training).
*   *Intent -> Action*: **3.3.2 Context Engineering** (Prompting Agents).

### 4.4 Meta-Layer
*   **Decisions & History**: Hiring Committees, Promo Packets.
*   **Situational Awareness**: **11.2.2 Engagement Surveys**, eNPS.
*   **Procedures**: Employment Contracts, Agent System Prompts ("You are a helpful assistant").
*   **Security**: Background Checks, **9.2.2 Internal Threat Monitoring**.
*   **Philosophy**: DEI Statement, **1.2.2 AI Ethics/Rights**.

---

## 5. Organizational Capital (Social & Structural)
*The connective tissue, trust, and shared consciousness.*

### 5.1 External (In/Out)
*   **External**: **4.2.1 Brand Identity** (Market Perception), **10.1 Ecosystem** (Trust Network).
*   **Extruder**: **20. Output Transducer** (PR, Comms).

### 5.2 Internal (Flow/Store)
*   **Storage**: **8.1.2 Knowledge Base** (Notion/Obsidian), Institutional Memory.
*   **Distributor**: **11.2.1 Internal Comms** (All-Hands, Slack).

### 5.3 Capital Transforms
*   *Values -> Behavior*: **11.2.2 Culture Rituals** (Reinforcing norms).
*   *Experience -> Wisdom*: **16. Associator** (Post-Mortems, Retrospectives).

### 5.4 Meta-Layer
*   **Decisions & History**: **6.1.1 Corporate Strategy**, Meeting Minutes.
*   **Situational Awareness**: Market Sentiment Analysis, Glassdoor Reviews.
*   **Procedures**: Org Chart, Decision Matrix (RACI).
*   **Security**: Reputation Management, Crisis Comms.
*   **Philosophy**: **1.2.2 Core Values**, Mission Statement, Doctrine.
