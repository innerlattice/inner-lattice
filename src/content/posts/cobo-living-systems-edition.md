> **Thesis**: The organization is a living system. Its capabilities are not "Departments" (like Marketing or Engineering) but **Biological Subsystems** (like Ingestor, Decider, Producer) operating across four planes.

---

## Plane 1: Infrastructure (The Container)
*The passive structures that hold, transport, and protect the system.*

### 2. Boundary (Protection Capability)
*The membrane that filters inputs and protects integrity.*
*   **9.1.2 Infrastructure Security**: Threat monitoring, Identity governance.
*   **9.2.2 Safety & Moderation**: Abuse detection, Policy enforcement.
*   **7.2.2 Corporate Protection**: Legal entity protection, IP/Patent defense.
*   **9.2.1 Compliance**: Certification audits (SOC2), Regulatory alignment.

### 3. Ingestor (Intake Capability)
*Bringing matter and energy across the boundary.*
*   **7.1.3 Treasury (Inbound)**: Cash management, Capital markets (Ingesting Energy).
*   **11.1.1 Talent Acquisition**: Sourcing, Assessment (Ingesting Human Matter).
*   **6.2.1 Procurement**: Vendor selection, Asset purchasing (Ingesting Physical Matter).
*   **4.3.2 Inbound Triage (SDR)**: Processing inbound leads (Ingesting Potential Energy).

### 4. Distributor (Transport Capability)
*Moving matter, energy, and information to components.*
*   **7.1.1 Capital Distribution (FP&A)**: Budget allocation, Financial modeling.
*   **8.1.1 Data Pipelines**: Pipeline construction, Schema modeling (Moving Info).
*   **6.2.2 Environment Logistics**: Workspace management, Asset distribution.
*   **3.2.2 Network Infrastructure**: Network config, Cluster management.

### 7. Storage (Reservoir Capability)
*Retaining matter and energy for later use.*
*   **8.1.2 Data Governance**: Data cataloging, Quality assurance (Storing Info).
*   **7.2.1 Contract Management**: Commercial records (Storing Legal Obligation).
*   **3.2.2 Infrastructure as Code**: State management (Storing System State).

### 8. Extruder (Output Capability)
*Transmitting products and wastes out of the system.*
*   **3.2.1 Release Engineering**: Toolchain maintenance, Artifact publishing.
*   **11.1.1 Offboarding**: Alumni management, Deprovisioning.

---

## Plane 2: Machines & Agents (The Transformer)
*The active processors that sense, decide, build, and act.*

### 11. Input Transducer (Sensing Capability)
*Bringing information markers into the system.*
*   **1.1.1 Market Analysis**: Competitive intel, Macro-forecasting.
*   **2.2.1 User Research**: User interviews, Persona development.
*   **10.1.1 Ecosystem Sensing**: Tech partnership scoping.

### 15. Decoder (Translation Capability)
*Translating external info into private system code.*
*   **2.2.2 Specification**: PRD writing, Acceptance criteria definition (Need -> Spec).
*   **7.2.1 Commercial Legal**: MSA redaction (Deal -> Contract).
*   **7.1.2 Accounting**: Tax filing, Audit prep (Event -> GAAP Code).

### 16. Associator (Learning Capability)
*Forming enduring associations and patterns.*
*   **8.2.2 Advanced Analytics**: Predictive modeling, Causal inference.
*   **1.1.1 Scenario Planning**: Correlating trends to outcomes.
*   **3.3.1 Model Operations**: Training pipelines, Vector indexing.

### 18. Decider (Control Capability)
*Executive guidance and resource control.*
*   **1.1.2 Goal Setting**: OKRs, KPI targets.
*   **1.1.3 Resource Allocation**: Portfolio management, Capital efficiency.
*   **2.1.1 Product Roadmapping**: Feature prioritization (RICE/WSJF).
*   **6.1.1 Corporate Strategy**: Board reporting, Strategic pillars.

### 19. Encoder (Formatting Capability)
*Translating private code into public output.*
*   **2.3.1 User Experience (UX)**: IA mapping, User flows.
*   **2.3.2 Interaction Design**: UI design, Modality switching.
*   **4.2.1 Product Marketing**: Positioning, Messaging (Spec -> Story).
*   **3.3.2 Context Engineering**: System instruction design (Intent -> Prompt).

### 6. Producer (Synthesis Capability)
*Forming stable associations (Building things).*
*   **3.4.1 Feature Build**: API implementation, UI component build.
*   **3.1.1 System Design**: RFC creation, Domain modeling.
*   **4.2.2 Demand Generation**: Campaign creation, SEO optimization.
*   **5.1.2 Solutions Engineering**: Custom integration, Workflow opt.

### 20. Output Transducer (Broadcast Capability)
*Transmitting information markers to the environment.*
*   **4.3.1 Sales Execution**: Discovery, Proposal, Closing.
*   **10.1.3 Developer Relations**: External advocacy, Documentation.
*   **11.2.1 Internal Comms**: Organizational alignment (All-hands).
*   **5.2.2 Support**: Incident resolution, Self-service curation.

### 5. Converter (Adaptation Capability)
*Changing inputs into forms useful for the system.*
*   **11.1.2 Learning & Development**: Competency mapping, Coaching (Human -> Employee).
*   **5.1.1 Implementation**: Technical onboarding (Buyer -> User).
*   **4.1.1 Funnel Operations**: Lead scoring, CRM architecture (Traffic -> Lead).

### 14. Timer (Coordination Capability)
*Coordinating processes in time.*
*   **6.1.2 Program Management**: Cross-functional coord, Change mgmt.
*   **3.2.3 Reliability (SRE)**: SLO/SLI management, Uptime.

### 12. Internal Transducer (Monitoring Capability)
*Reporting on internal states.*
*   **8.2.1 Business Intelligence**: Dashboard viz, Metric definition.
*   **3.1.2 Tech Health**: Debt backlog, Dependency governance.
*   **11.2.2 Employee Engagement**: Ritual facilitation, Recognition.

## Plane 3: Artifacts (The Flows)
*The matter, energy, and information being processed.*

*   **Capital Flow**: Cash, Budget, Credit.
*   **Information Flow**: MQLs, PRDs, Tickets, Slack Messages.
*   **Matter Flow**: Hardware assets, Human candidates.

## Plane 4: Meta-Artifacts (The Blueprint)
*The genetic code guiding the agents.*

*   **1.2.2 Technology Ethics**: Responsible use guidelines.
*   **3.1.1 Architecture RFCs**: System blueprints.
*   **1.1.2 OKRs**: Strategic goals.
*   **6.1.1 Operating Policies**: The rules of the game.
