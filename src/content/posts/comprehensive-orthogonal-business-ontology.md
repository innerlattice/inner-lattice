	# The Comprehensive Orthogonal Business Ontology (COBO)

> **Purpose**: To provide a mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive (MECE) map of every function within a standard technology company. This ontology serves as the "source code" for automating the enterprise.

## 1. Ontology Architecture
To ensure orthogonality (no overlap), we categorize functions by their **Outcome** and **Object of Manipulation**.

*   **L1: Domain** (The high-level "Swarm" or Department)
*   **L2: Capability** (A grouping of related conceptual abilities)
*   **L3: Process** (A sequence of actions to achieve a result)
*   **L4: Atomic Action** (The indivisible unit of work, automatable by an agent)

---

## 2. The 11 Domains (L1)

1.  **Executive (The Brain)**: Strategy, Governance, resource allocation.
2.  **Product (The Definition)**: Defining *what* to build based on market signal.
3.  **Engineering (The Factory)**: Building the artifact defined by Product.
4.  **Growth (The Engine)**: Acquiring capital and customers.
5.  **Success (The Outcomes)**: Ensuring value delivery and expansion.
6.  **Operations (The Backbone)**: Business orchestration and efficiency.
7.  **Finance & Legal (The Ledger & Resource Allocation)**: Maintaining fiscal and juridical integrity.
8.  **People & Culture (The Organism)**: Managing human and agentic capital.
9.  **Data & Analytics (The Nervous System)**: Sensing, processing, and distributing intelligence.
10. **Security & Trust (The Immune System)**: Protecting integrity and ensuring safety.
11. **Partnerships (The Ecosystem)**: Expanding reach through alliances.

*(Note: Infrastructure is now largely subsumed by Engineering (Platform) and Security/IT, but distinct L1 status is reserved for IaaS/PaaS companies where it is the product).*

## 3. Deep Dive Hierarchies

### 1. Domain: Executive (The Brain)
**Goal**: Make high-quality decisions to maximize enterprise value.

*   **1.1 Strategy & Planning**
    *   1.1.1 **Market Analysis** [Evo: Custom]: 1.1.1.1 Gather competitive intel, 1.1.1.2 Scenario Planning, 1.1.1.3 Macro-economic forecasting.
    *   1.1.2 **Goal Setting** [Evo: Custom]: 1.1.2.1 Define Objectives (OKRs), 1.1.2.2 Set KPI targets, 1.1.2.3 Strategic Pillar alignment.
    *   1.1.3 **Resource Allocation** [Evo: Custom]: 1.1.3.1 Capital efficiency modeling, 1.1.3.2 Portfolio management.
*   **1.2 Governance & Ethics**
    *   1.2.1 **Corporate Governance** [Evo: Product]: 1.2.1.1 Board reporting, 1.2.1.2 Stakeholder management.
    *   1.2.2 **Technology Ethics** [Evo: Genesis]: 1.2.2.1 Responsible use guidelines, 1.2.2.2 Ethics review board (Human-in-the-loop).

## 4. The Functional Interface Map
How the domains speak to each other (The "API" of the company).

| From Domain | To Domain | Artifact Transferred (The "Payload") |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Executive** | **Product** | Strategic Pillars, Budget Constraints |
| **Product** | **Engineering** | PRD (Spec), Design Assets, Priorities |
| **Engineering** | **Growth** | Release Note, Commercially Ready Product |
| **Growth** | **Sales** | MQL (Marketing Qualified Lead) |
| **Sales** | **Success** | Signed Contract, Handover Notes |
| **Success** | **Product** | User Feedback, Feature Requests, Bug Reports |
| **Operations** | **All** | Paychecks, Legal Protection, Policy |

## 5. Usage in Automation
To automate a company, traverse this ontology:
1.  **Select a Node** (e.g., 8.1.2 Governance).
2.  **Define the Agent** (e.g., "Data Steward Agent").
3.  **Map Inputs** (e.g., Unclassified Table Schema).
4.  **Map Outputs** (e.g., Classified Metadata Tag).
5.  **Codify Process** (e.g., The prompts and tool chains to execute 8.1.2.1).
